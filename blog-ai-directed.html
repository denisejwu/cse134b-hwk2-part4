Mexican Chochoyotes with Chicken Soup

The Origin

Geographically they are tied to the Zapotec and Mixtec culinary tradition in Oaxaca, where they are called ombligitos ("little belly buttons"). The dumplings are small cor-masa balls with a signature thumbprint indention in the center. They are dropped directly into hot broths typically to thicken the soup by releaseing starches as they cook.

Why People Eat Chochoyotes

The reason many people typically eat chochoyotes is to make the dish more filling. The added dumplings turn a light broth into a satisfying substanital meal. The small pocket in the chochoyte allows to hold the surrounding broth givng the chochoyte more flavor.
