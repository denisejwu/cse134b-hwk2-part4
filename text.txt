   Geographically they are tied to the Zapotec and Mixtec culinary tradition
               in Oaxaca, where they are called ombligitos ("little belly buttons").
               The dumplings are small cor-masa balls with a signature thumbprint indention in the center. 
               They are dropped directly into hot broths typically to thicken the soup by releaseing starches as they cook.
The reason many people typically eat chochoyotes is to make the dish more filling 
                The added dumplings turn a light broth into a satisfying substanital meal. The small pocket
                in the chochoyte allows to hold the surrounding broth givng the chochoyte more flavor
